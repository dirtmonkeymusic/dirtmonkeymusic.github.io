Refunds & support

Need a hand? Email support@dirtmonkey.music with the email you paid with. A real person answers, usually within a day or two.

Your license

  • One key works on up to 2 computers, Mac or Windows.
  • New computer? Deactivate the old one inside the plugin to free its slot. Lost access to the old one? Email us and we'll reset it.
  • Lost your key? Email us from the address you paid with and we'll resend it.

Try before you buy

  • Paid plugins come with a free 7-day demo, one per computer. Meter and Frequalizer are free and have no demo.

Refunds

  • Not happy? Email us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund you in full.
  • A full refund turns off the license key for that purchase.
  • If you open a dispute with your bank or PayPal instead, the key stays on while it's looked at. If the dispute goes against the purchase, the key is turned off.

Rent-to-own (The Everything Suite)

  • Cancel any time by emailing support@dirtmonkey.music. The plugins lock until you pick it back up.
  • Monthly payments already made aren't refunded, but they always count toward owning it. Come back with the same email and you pick up where you left off.
  • After payment 10 it's yours forever, and the payments stop on their own.
