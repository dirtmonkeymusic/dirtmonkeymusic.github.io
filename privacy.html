Privacy Policy

Dirt Monkey: Skwish Run (iPhone app) · Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy covers the Dirt Monkey iPhone app, including the game Skwish Run (the "App"). The short version: the App does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The App has no accounts or sign-up, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage data or any other personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, unlocked secret tracks and game settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the App.

Internet use

The App downloads the public Dirt Monkey tour schedule from Bandsintown so it can show upcoming shows. This request does not include any personal information about you. Like any website, Bandsintown's servers receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your phone connects to them; see the Bandsintown privacy policy.

Links to other websites

Some buttons open other websites, such as the official Dirt Monkey merch store (dirtmonkey.bassthreadsmerch.com), ticket sellers, Bandsintown and Patreon. Merch store links include labels that tell the store which button in the App was tapped (for example "game_over"); these labels do not identify you. Those websites are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. Anything you do there, such as placing an order, is governed by their policies, not this one.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
