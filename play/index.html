How to play

Hit the line, feed the combo

  • Hit the notes. Each lane has a key: D F J K from left to right (the arrow keys work too). On a phone or tablet, tap the lane. Press when the note touches the glowing line.
  • Timing counts. Dead on is Perfect for 300 points. A little early or late is Good for 120. Too far off, or no press at all, is a Miss. Tapping an empty lane costs nothing.
  • Build a combo. Every 10 hits in a row adds to your multiplier, up to four times the points. A miss resets it, and Skwish winces.
  • Survive the drop. Watch for DROP! The notes get busy, the screen pulses with the bass, and Skwish goes wild.
  • Chase your best. Your best score stays saved on this device. Pause any time with Esc, P, or the Pause button, and switch the sound with M or the Sound button.
