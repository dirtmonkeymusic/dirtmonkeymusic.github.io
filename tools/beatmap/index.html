In the game

The beatmap

Play with clicks plays the track with a tick on every note. If the ticks sit on the hits, the BPM and offset are right.

  1. Put the track in the site at /assets/audio/.
  2. Swap in the downloaded beatmap.js at js/beatmap.js, or paste the track block over the lines between >>> TRACK and <<< TRACK.
  3. Open the game and play it. Tweak any note by hand in js/beatmap.js if you want.
