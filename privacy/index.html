Dirt Monkey App: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026.

This policy explains what the Dirt Monkey iOS app ("the app") collects. The app is published by Dirt Monkey Touring, Inc. ("we").

What we collect

  • Scoreboard (optional). If you sign in with Apple to join the online Skwish Run scoreboard, we store an anonymous Apple-provided user identifier, the nickname you choose, and your scores (best run, bananas) in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Your nickname and scores are visible to other players. We never receive your name or email from Sign in with Apple.
  • On your device only. Game progress, settings and downloaded images are kept on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • Purchases. If in-app purchases are offered, Apple processes payment; we do not see your payment details.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, no selling or sharing of data.

Links

The app links to outside sites (Patreon, music services, merch, tour tickets). Their privacy policies apply there.

Reporting and removal

Long-press a scoreboard name to report it. To delete your scoreboard data, email dirtmonkey@mail.grokbot.com and we'll remove it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes / contact

We'll update this page if anything changes. Questions: dirtmonkey@mail.grokbot.com.
