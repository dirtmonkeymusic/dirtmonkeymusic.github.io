Dirt Monkey: Skwish Run – Support

Need help with the Dirt Monkey iPhone app or the game Skwish Run? Email dirtmonkeymusic@gmail.com and tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened.

Quick tips

  • Jumping: tap for a small hop, press and hold for a big jump.
  • Golden Skwish: collect 100 bananas to go golden.
  • No sound? Check the mute button in the game and your iPhone's ring/silent switch and volume.
  • Lost progress? Scores and unlocks are saved only on your phone and are removed if you delete the app.
