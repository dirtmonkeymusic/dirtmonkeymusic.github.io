Dirt Monkey: Monkey Run – Support

Need help with the Dirt Monkey iPhone app or the game Monkey Run? Email dirtmonkey@mail.grokbot.com and tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened.

Quick tips

  • Jumping: tap for a small hop, press and hold for a big jump.
  • Golden Monkey: collect 100 bananas to go golden.
  • No sound? Check the mute button in the game and your iPhone's ring/silent switch and volume.
  • Lost progress? Your game progress and unlocks are saved on your phone, and deleting the app removes them. If you sign in with Apple, your optional Monkey Run scoreboard scores are stored in iCloud.
